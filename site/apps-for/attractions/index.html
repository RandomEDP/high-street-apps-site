Questions

Things people ask.

Does the map need GPS on every path?

No. The map is a drawn plan of your site; the phone's location simply shows where the visitor is on it.

Will it work with my ticketing system?

Ticket links work with almost anything. A deeper link, so passes and tickets show up in the app automatically, depends on your provider, and I check that in the free concept.

What about signal on site?

The map, times and pass are stored on the phone, so they work without signal.

Can we update talk times on the day?

Yes, from a simple sheet or your website, and visitors see the change straight away.
