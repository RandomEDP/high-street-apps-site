Questions

Things people ask.

Is an app worth it for a single café?

It can be, if a big share of your takings comes from regulars. A loyalty card that brings each regular back one extra time a month usually covers the upkeep many times over. The free concept is there so you can judge it before spending anything.

Will it work with my till?

Many card machines and tills can share orders and loyalty with an app. I'll check what yours supports while I make the free concept, and tell you plainly if it can't.

Will customers really download it?

Regulars will, especially when the app is the loyalty card. A sign by the till and a free drink for downloading does most of the work.

How do I change the menu?

The app reads the menu from the same source as your website, so you update it in one place. Upkeep also covers small changes, if you'd rather I did it.
