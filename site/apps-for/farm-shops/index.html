Questions

Things people ask.

Can it take Christmas orders?

Yes. Customers choose what they want and a collection slot. On Standard they reserve and pay as they do now; on Custom they can pay in the app.

We're a small team. Who keeps it updated?

It reads from your website, so there's usually nothing extra to do. Upkeep covers small changes when you'd rather hand them over.

Is it worth it for a seasonal business?

Seasonal is where push messages earn their keep: they bring people back for each new crop, not just once a year.

Does it need good signal?

Key information like opening times and directions is saved on the phone, so it still works down a lane with patchy signal.
