Questions

Things people ask.

We already have a plastic loyalty card. Can the app replace it?

Often, yes. If your till system can share loyalty data, the app becomes the card. If not, the app can show the member number for scanning.

Can it sell grotto tickets?

Yes. On Standard it links to your current ticketing; on Custom tickets are bought in the app.

Do older customers use apps?

Many do, and the app is an extra, not a replacement. Everything still works in store as it does today.

Can we change offers ourselves?

Yes, offers and events update from your website or a simple sheet, and upkeep covers anything bigger.
