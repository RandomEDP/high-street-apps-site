Questions

Things people ask.

Will it work with my booking system?

Most booking systems used by gyms and climbing walls either share their data or have booking links an app can open. I check yours while making the free concept.

My booking provider already has an app. Why have my own?

Their app shows every gym that uses them, under their brand. Yours shows only you, with your routes, your classes and your offers, and the push messages come from you.

Can members pay for memberships in the app?

Yes, on the Custom tier. Many places start with Standard and keep payments in their existing system.

Does it work on Android?

Standard includes an Android version or a web app, so every member is covered.
