Questions

Things people ask.

Will it work with my table booking system?

Most pub and restaurant booking systems can be linked from an app, and some share availability directly. I check yours in the free concept.

Do people really want an app for a pub?

Your regulars do, if it gives them something: first word on gig tickets, a loyalty reward, or booking the good table on Sunday.

We're a music venue, not a pub. Does this fit?

Yes. Listings, tickets and push alerts when a show goes on sale are exactly what a venue app is for.

Can the app send messages to everyone?

Only to people who've said yes to notifications, and you choose when. Used sparingly, they're the most direct way to fill a quiet night.
