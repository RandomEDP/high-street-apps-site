App concept for Jubilee Pool

Your lido, in every swimmer's pocket.

A quick concept of how Jubilee Pool's swim slots, passes, classes and café could work as one iPhone app.

  • Live pool temperatures and swim slots
  • Swim passes on the phone
  • Wellness classes and events, booked in a tap
  • Café orders after the swim
